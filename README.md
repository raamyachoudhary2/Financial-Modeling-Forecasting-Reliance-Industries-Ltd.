# Financial-Modeling-Forecasting-Reliance-Industries-Ltd.
An Excel-based financial model of Reliance Industries Ltd. that combines 10 years of historical financial statements, a detailed ratio analysis, and a Sales, EBITDA and EPS forecast.

What I Did -

I built a financial model of Reliance Industries Ltd. in Excel that covers ten years of the company's history, from FY2016 to FY2025. The model brings together the income statement, the balance sheet and the cash flow statement, evaluates the company through a detailed ratio analysis, and forecasts its Sales, EBITDA and EPS for the next six years, from FY2026 to FY2031.

How I Did It -

I collected the company's financial data from Screener.in and placed it in a data sheet. I then built the historical statements on a separate sheet using linked formulas, so that every figure traces back to the source data and updates automatically. For the ratio analysis, I calculated growth rates, profit margins, returns such as ROE, ROCE and ROA, interest coverage, turnover ratios, working capital days and cash flow ratios across all ten years, so each ratio can be read as a trend instead of a single number. For the forecast, I used Excel's FORECAST.LINEAR function, which fits a straight-line trend to the historical figures and extends it forward to estimate Sales, EBITDA and EPS.

What Problem It Solves -

A company's annual reports contain a lot of numbers, and it is hard to judge from them alone whether the business is becoming more profitable, more efficient or riskier over time. This model puts ten years of data in one place and turns it into margins, returns and efficiency ratios that show how the company has performed. The forecast then gives a reasonable view of where Sales, EBITDA and EPS are heading, which supports valuation analysis and investment study. The forecast is a simple trend projection and not a prediction driven by detailed assumptions, so it is best used as a starting point for analysis.

Repository Contents -

This repository contains the Excel workbook with the data sheet, the historical statements, the ratio analysis and the forecasting sheet, along with screenshots of the main sheets.
